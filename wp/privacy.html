Terms used in this policy carry the same meanings as in our Terms and Conditions.

Installing and using Turnguys mobile applications may involve the collection and processing of some of your data for legitimate purposes. Turnguys and its third-party partners collect data to:

Error and diagnostic data

If an error occurs while you are using the app, we collect diagnostic information from your phone through third-party products. This information, referred to as log data, may include:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address and model.
  • The operating system version and the app's configuration when you use the Service.
  • The date and time of your use of the Service, along with other statistics.

These service providers have access to your personal information to complete the tasks we assign to them. They are required not to disclose that information or use it for any other purpose.

Children's privacy

The Service is not directed to anyone younger than 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under that age. If we learn that a child under 13 has given us personal information, we immediately delete it from our servers.

If you are a parent or guardian and know that your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at turnguysgames@gmail.com so we can take the necessary action.
